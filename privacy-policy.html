Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

The short version

Sector Light does not collect any data. It has no server, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

What is stored on your device

Your best result on each chart and your settings (sound, haptics, reduced flashing). Deleting the app removes them.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple’s Game Center, the app posts your score for a chart, and your total, to its leaderboards, where they appear next to your Game Center nickname. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy, and you can sign out of it in your device’s Settings. The game is fully playable without it.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes the payment; the app receives only confirmation that the purchase exists. We never see your name, email or payment details.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

None. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code and does not use the App Tracking Transparency framework.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
