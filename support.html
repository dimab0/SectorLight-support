How do I turn a light?

Tap the lighthouse to select it, then drag anywhere on the chart: the beam follows your finger in 15-degree steps. The turn buttons under the chart do the same one step at a time.

Why did my vessel stop?

It reached a gate that was dark. A vessel waits at a dark gate until a light shines on it. If no light reaches the gate at all, the voyage ends at once; if it waits past the tide, it ends then.

Why was my vessel lost?

A red sector touched it. Red sectors turn with their lights and flash with them. Aim the white beam where it is needed and the red where nobody sails, or time the crossing for when the light is dark.

How is the score worked out?

10,000, less 250 for each adjustment, less 1 for each tick (a twentieth of a second) the crossing takes. Repeated changes to the same setting of one light count as one adjustment. Match the chart's par for three stars.

I bought the full atlas but the charts are locked.

Open Settings (the gear on the title screen) or the unlock screen and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

My score is not on the leaderboard.

Leaderboards use Game Center. Sign in under Settings › Game Center on your device. Scores you earned while signed out are kept on the device and sent the next time you open the game signed in.

The flashing is uncomfortable.

Turn on Reduced flashing in Settings: dark phases dim instead of going black. The rules do not change.
